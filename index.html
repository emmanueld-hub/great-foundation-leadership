Who We Are

A strong future starts with a strong foundation.

We exist to help boys and young men become capable, confident, purpose-driven leaders through mentorship, education, and a community that stays present throughout the journey.

Our work is organized around three foundational pillars: Development, Empowerment, and Community.

The Development Journey

Growing with each new horizon.

Participants progress through age-appropriate stages designed to deepen purpose, leadership, character, and readiness for life after high school.

01

Foundation

Build fundamentals in character, purpose, leadership, literacy, and belonging.

02

Formation

Advance through focused leadership, decision-making, communication, and life-readiness experiences.

03

Capstone

Complete a service project, celebrate achievement, and step into a continuing leadership and mentor pipeline.

Meet the Executive Director.

Emmanuel S. Desmolieres, CLTC®

President & Executive Director

Emmanuel S. Desmolieres is a financial educator, financial planner, insurance strategist, and youth-development advocate. He leads Great Foundation Leadership Program with a focus on helping young men build strong foundations in literacy, leadership, character, financial capability, responsible technology use, and life readiness.

His work centers on practical education, mentorship, and creating pathways that help young people grow into capable, confident, purpose-driven adults.
