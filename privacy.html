Website Policy

Privacy Policy.

How Great Foundation Leadership Program handles information submitted through this website.

Effective August 20, 2026

Great Foundation Leadership Program Inc. ("Great Foundation," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of the individuals and families who visit our website, express interest in our programs, volunteer, mentor, partner with us, or otherwise contact the organization.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through website forms, including your name, email address, phone number, organization, relationship to a prospective participant, program interests, and information included in messages you send to us.

When a parent or guardian submits an interest form for a young person, we may also receive limited information about that prospective participant that is reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry and evaluate program interest.

How We Use Information

We may use information submitted through the website to:

  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Communicate with parents, guardians, prospective participants, mentors, volunteers, donors, and community partners.
  • Evaluate interest in Great Foundation programs and opportunities.
  • Administer forms, events, partnerships, and organizational communications.
  • Protect the security and appropriate operation of the website.

Information About Minors

Great Foundation serves youth, but this public website is intended to support communication with parents, guardians, volunteers, mentors, partners, and the broader community. Parents or legal guardians should submit inquiries involving children under 13.

We do not knowingly use this website to collect personal information directly from a child under 13 without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information through the website, they may contact us and request that the information be reviewed or deleted.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, process website forms, maintain technology systems, or perform other services on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of Great Foundation, our participants, or others.

Website Forms and Service Providers

Information submitted through forms may be processed or stored by third-party technology providers that support the website and our communications. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy and security practices.

Cookies and Technical Information

Our hosting and technology providers may automatically receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, or basic usage information. This information may be used for security, website performance, and analytics.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate organizational records, or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact Great Foundation to ask about personal information you previously submitted through the website, request a correction, or request deletion when appropriate and legally permitted.

External Links

Our website may link to websites or services operated by other organizations. Great Foundation is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our programs, website, or legal obligations evolve. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.

Contact Us

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy may be submitted through our Contact page.

Note: This website policy is intended as a practical organizational policy and should be reviewed periodically as Great Foundation expands its programs, technology systems, and data practices.
