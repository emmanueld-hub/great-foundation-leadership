GR

Foundational Literacy

GreatReaders

GreatReaders serves boys in grades 5–8 through a book-based curriculum, one-on-one and small-group tutoring from trained Literacy Tutors, partnerships with local schools and libraries, and an AI-supported reading tool. The program is designed as an intervention-first literacy initiative that helps students strengthen foundational reading skills while building confidence and engagement with books.

FS

Financial Literacy

FinSmarts

FinSmarts equips young men with practical financial knowledge for everyday life. Programming introduces core money concepts such as earning, saving, banking, budgeting, credit, investing, entrepreneurship, protection, and responsible financial decision-making in age-appropriate ways.

AI Literacy

AI Literacy prepares young people to understand emerging artificial intelligence tools, use them responsibly, evaluate information thoughtfully, protect their privacy, and apply technology productively for learning, creativity, and future opportunity.
